About

I'm an electronic civil engineer with an M.Sc. in Electronic Engineering from Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María. My background is in signal processing, estimation theory and automatic control, and I now apply it to AI.

Since 2021 I've worked on computer vision for industrial applications. At Kauel I built distributed Edge/Cloud vision solutions, including volume-estimation services, along with the APIs and Docker containers to put models into production. At ETT Transferencia de Tecnologías I worked on detection, multi-object tracking and 3D reconstruction for SAG mills in mining.

I'm currently at VSAAS, doing R&D on intelligent video-surveillance platforms. I'm also developing a line of research on agentic systems: architectures that combine visual perception, contextual memory and LLM reasoning to handle events autonomously.
